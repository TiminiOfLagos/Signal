Why Signal

We heard a room nobody built

The talent was never the bottleneck. The room was.

There are producers in Surulere making finished records on a laptop, a thirty-dollar interface and a mattress nailed to the wall. The records are good. What they do not have is eight uninterrupted hours in a room where the low end tells the truth.

So we built
the rooms.
Then we started
giving them away.

Signal is not a competition and there is no trophy. It is a year of access — a room with a door that locks, a budget that clears, and an engineer who picks up the phone on a Sunday.

Sessions 01 and 02 ran out of two rooms in Lagos. Sixteen residents came through. Between them they released nine projects, four of which charted, and two are now engineering for other people full time. That is the entire measure we care about: did the work get made, and did the person keep making it.

For Sessions 03 we are opening outside Nigeria for the first time, and adding a lane for the people who make everybody else sound good.

“I had the songs for two years. I just never had a room quiet enough to hear what was wrong with them.” Ify O. — Resident, Sessions 02

Two lanes.
Pick the one
that is actually
yours.

The Residency Lane is for artists and producers with a body of work — rough is fine, finished is not required, but there has to be a body of it. Twelve seats across six genres.

The Engineering Lane is for the people behind the glass: mixers, masterers, live engineers, room builders. Four seats, same stipend, same year of access.

Applying to both is allowed. Getting both is not.

Start the application Closes 09 Oct 2026 · 23:59 WAT
  • 01Be 18 or older. There is no upper limit — Sessions 02 had a fifty-four-year-old resident and he made the best record of the cohort.
  • 02Be based in, or able to work from, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya or South Africa for the length of the residency.
  • 03Send three tracks you would put your name on. Rough mixes are fine. We would rather hear a demo that means something than a master that does not.
  • 04Own what you send, or have the right to send it. We check.
  • 05For the Engineering Lane: two credits, or two things you mixed that we can listen to. Bedroom credits count.

Two lanes, sixteen seats, one year of room time. The whole thing starts with three tracks.

Three tracks. No cover letter, no showreel, no pitch deck. Tell us where you are and what you are trying to make, and let the music do the rest.

We listen to every single submission, all the way through, at least once. That is a promise we have kept for three years and it is the reason applications close when they do.
